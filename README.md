## 🌟 Overview

**BIS Intelligence — Manak Mitra** is an AI-powered intelligent assistant designed to simplify access to information related to the **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)**.

The platform provides a conversational interface for discovering information about:

- 🇮🇳 Indian Standards
- 📋 BIS Certification Schemes
- 🏭 Product Certification
- 💍 Hallmarking and HUID
- 🧪 BIS-recognized laboratories
- 👥 Consumer-related BIS services
- 📚 Standards and compliance information
- 🌐 Multilingual BIS assistance

The system combines **Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), semantic search, structured retrieval, multilingual NLP and evidence verification** to provide responses grounded in relevant BIS information.

### Core Principle

> **Retrieve relevant evidence first, generate the answer from that evidence, and avoid unsupported responses when sufficient evidence is unavailable.**

---

# 🎯 Problem

BIS information is distributed across different portals, documents, standards and service pages.

For MSMEs, startups, consumers, students and researchers, finding the correct information can require:

- Searching multiple BIS portals
- Reading lengthy documents
- Understanding technical terminology
- Identifying applicable standards
- Understanding certification requirements
- Finding hallmarking information
- Locating relevant laboratory or consumer information

Traditional search systems can return large amounts of information without understanding the user's intent.

### 💡 Our Solution

**Manak Mitra** brings these information workflows into one intelligent platform.

Instead of manually searching multiple sources, users can ask questions naturally and receive:

✅ Relevant information  
✅ Context-aware answers  
✅ Source-backed responses  
✅ Multilingual interaction  
✅ Evidence-based results  
✅ Safe handling of unsupported questions
